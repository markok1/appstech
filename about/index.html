Our

Journey

We are a leading staffing firm based in Dallas, Texas, with over two decades of experience in delivering exceptional workforce solutions. Specializing in Healthcare, IT, and Non-IT staffing, we have successfully partnered with the State of Georgia, State of Illinois, and State of Texas to meet their hiring needs.

With a strong foundation built on expertise and reliability, we efficiently manage 1000+ W2 payrolls, ensuring smooth and compliant workforce operations. As we continue to expand, we are eager to collaborate with businesses seeking customized staffing solutions that drive growth and success.

At Apsstech, we specialize in managing the complete logistics of contingent staffing—so your operations remain efficient, compliant, and scalable. Every business is different, which is why we don’t believe in one-size-fits-all solutions. Instead, we take the time to understand your operational goals, risk parameters, and workforce demands to craft a customized staffing solution tailored to your needs.

Once deployed, our solutions are continuously evaluated and refined based on regulatory changes, client preferences, labor law updates, and evolving business conditions. This agile approach ensures your workforce strategy stays aligned with performance and compliance benchmarks.
